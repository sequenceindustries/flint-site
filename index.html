Questions

Good to know before you sign up.

Can the assistant say anything, or just what I approve?
Only what you've approved, word for word. Flint can understand a customer's question even when they phrase it in a new way, but it can only point at an answer you've already written and signed off. If nothing you've approved covers the question, it tells the customer it's passing them to you — it never improvises.
What happens when it can't answer something?
It hands the conversation to you, inside your own WhatsApp Business app, with a summary of what's been discussed, what's been collected so far, and why it's asking for you. You reply as yourself, and Flint automatically stays out of that conversation until you're done.
Do I need to be technical to set this up?
No. Setup is a guided interview about your business — what you offer, your hours, your prices, what you never promise. You review and approve the drafted answers, then switch it on. No code, no new app to learn.
Is there anything I pay besides the monthly plan?
Yes, two things. A once-off R499 setup fee when you join, which covers your onboarding chat and getting your first answers written and approved. And WhatsApp's own messaging fees from Meta once you're past the free monthly allowance, billed at cost with no markup from us — usually a few rand a month for a typical small business. We'll show you the real numbers before you commit to anything.
What about my customers' data and POPIA?
Customer conversations are encrypted, kept private to your business, and never used to train shared AI models. Sensitive details like ID numbers or card numbers are kept out of chat logs and summaries by default. We take a South African small business's compliance obligations seriously because we're one ourselves.
Can I change the approved answers later?
Yes, any time. Prices change, hours change, policies change — you edit the approved answer and it updates everywhere Flint uses it. Nothing is locked in at setup.
